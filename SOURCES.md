# Sources — the news of Monday 23 November 2026

Every story on the poster was reported on, or was unfolding on,
23 November 2026 — the front pages of that morning, assembled while
the day was still young in Asia-Pacific time zones. The masthead
names the day, and each claim on the page is kept short so it stays
faithful to the reporting. Anything phrased as "says" is a claim by
the side that made it, not a confirmed fact.

## Lead story — the Gulf war widens

- Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they struck U.S. air-defence
  radars at Bahrain's al-Riffa and Awali airbases with missiles and
  drones. Bahrain said the attacks were repelled, a fire broke out
  at a civilian industrial facility, and there were no casualties.
  A Houthi ballistic missile was launched at western Saudi Arabia;
  the Saudi-led coalition said it was intercepted. Tankers were hit
  in the Strait of Hormuz. Reported by Cutthroat News, Jamuna
  i-Desk, Archipelago Expat.
- Background from the days before: the Guards said they shot down
  two U.S. drones over the Strait of Hormuz and Qeshm Island on
  21 November, and tankers were hit by unidentified projectiles
  near Oman.
